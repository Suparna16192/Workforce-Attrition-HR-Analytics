# Workforce Attrition Analysis | Advanced Excel

## Project Overview

This project analyses employee workforce data using Microsoft Excel to understand attrition patterns, workforce trends, and key employee exit drivers.

The analysis focuses on monthly workforce movement, attrition rates, sub-department performance, work-from-office (WFO) vs work-from-home (WFH) attrition, and employee exit reasons.

The objective is to transform raw employee data into meaningful workforce insights that can support HR and management decision-making.

## Business Objectives

- Calculate monthly opening and closing headcount
- Analyse monthly employee attrition trends
- Compare voluntary and involuntary attrition
- Identify departments/sub-departments with higher attrition
- Compare WFO vs WFH attrition
- Identify the major employee exit reasons
- Evaluate whether early-tenure employees represent an attrition concern
- Develop actionable recommendations based on workforce trends

## Tools & Techniques

- Microsoft Excel
- Advanced Excel formulas
- Pivot Tables
- Data Cleaning
- Data Validation
- Conditional Formatting
- KPI Calculations
- Workforce/HR Analytics
- Dashboard & Data Visualization

## Key Analysis

### 1. Monthly Attrition Analysis

Calculated monthly workforce movement and attrition rates to identify changes in employee turnover over time.

### 2. Sub-Department Attrition

Compared attrition across sub-departments to identify areas with relatively higher employee turnover.

### 3. WFO vs WFH Attrition

Analysed attrition patterns based on employee work mode to identify differences between work-from-office and work-from-home employees.

### 4. Exit Reason Analysis

Analysed employee exit reasons to understand the primary drivers of workforce turnover.

### 5. Early-Tenure Attrition

Examined employee exits among newer employees to determine whether early-tenure attrition represents a workforce retention concern.

## Dashboard

- Voluntary vs Involuntary Attrition
- Sub-Department Attrition
- WFO vs WFH Attrition

## Key Insights

- Identified monthly changes in workforce attrition and headcount.
- Compared voluntary and involuntary employee exits.
- Identified sub-departments with relatively higher attrition.
- Compared attrition between WFO and WFH employees.
- Identified the leading employee exit reasons.
- Evaluated early-tenure attrition to identify potential retention concerns.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, recommendations were developed around:

- Monitoring high-attrition sub-departments
- Investigating key employee exit drivers
- Strengthening retention initiatives for early-tenure employees
- Monitoring workforce trends through regular HR reporting
- Using workforce KPIs to support proactive decision-making

## Project Outcome

This project demonstrates the use of Excel for workforce data cleaning, analysis, KPI reporting, visualization, and business insight generation.

It also demonstrates how raw employee data can be transformed into structured workforce reporting to support data-driven HR decision-making.
